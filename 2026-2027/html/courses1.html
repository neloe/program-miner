    Credit Hours: 3

    Prerequisite(s): ACCT 51202  with a minimum grade of C.

    Prerequisite(s): ACCT 51201  and MATH 17114  each with a minimum grade of C.

  • AGRI 03102 - Principles of Microeconomics in Agriculture

    Credit Hours: 3
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR ECON 102A

    Principles of production, supply and demand are applied to economic problems of agriculture and agriculturally related industries. Emphasis is placed on understanding the theoretical underpinnings of decision analysis within the biological, institutional and structural parameters of the agricultural sector.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP


    https://journeytocollege.mo.gov/finish/transfer-credit/core-42/

  • AGRI 03301 - Professional Selling in Agriculture

    Credit Hours: 3
    The course is designed to introduce students to professional sales techniques used in the agriculture industry. Focus will be placed on developing professional selling techniques for specialized agricultural products and characteristics of the customers purchasing these specialized products. Topics include methods of selling, steps and techniques in the selling process, customer service, sales ethics, consumer behavior concepts, and sales management in agriculture.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03102  
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

    Credit Hours: 3

  • AGRI 03307 - Farm Business Records

    Credit Hours: 3
    An application and examination of accounting principles applied to the issues and unique situations that occur in the agricultural industry. Double-entry accounting is emphasized. An accrual-adjusted system based on cash-basis accounting which is the most prevalent accounting system used in agricultural operations is utilized to provide more accurate information to measure profitability.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03304  and ACCT 51201  or permission of instructor.
Credit Hours: 3

Credit Hours: 3

  • AGRI 03314 - Applied AC/DC Electricity

    Credit Hours: 2
    This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the principles of alternating current (AC) and direct current (DC) electricity. Through theoretical concepts and practical applications, students will develop a strong foundation in electrical circuits, components, and analytical techniques. Topics covered include circuit laws, voltage, current, power, capacitance, inductance, impedance, and basic circuit construction and analysis.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • AGRI 03315 - Applied Mechatronics

    Credit Hours: 2
    This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the principles of mechatronics. Through theoretical concepts and practical applications, students will develop a strong foundation in mechanical systems, sensors, and control systems. Topics covered included integration of mechanical, electrical, and computer systems, programming microcontrollers and programmable logic controllers (PLC), and analyzing and troubleshooting mechatronic prototypes and systems.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • AGRI 03316 - Applied Mechanical Systems

    Credit Hours: 2
    This course is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of mechanical drive systems, focusing on the principles, design, and applications of belt, chain, and gear mechanisms. Through a combination of theoretical concepts and practical applications, students will gain the knowledge and skills necessary to analyze, design, and troubleshoot various mechanical drive systems.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • AGRI 03317 - Applied Robotics

    Credit Hours: 2
    This course provides an introduction to the field of robotics, covering fundamental concepts, theories, and applications. It explores the interdisciplinary nature of robotics, combining aspects of mechanics, electrical systems, computer science, and artificial intelligence. Through theoretical studies, practical demonstrations, and hands-on projects, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of robotic systems, their components, programming, and real-world applications.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • AGRI 03319 - Principles of Facility Management

    Credit Hours: 2
    A course designed to explore the impact of functionality, safety, and efficiency use of facilities through the integration of space, process, technology, and people. Students explore facility management concepts including functions of facility management and International Facility Management Association (IFMA) core competencies.

Credit Hours: 2

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03234  or AGRI 03232  or permission of instructor.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03432 - Soil and Water Conservation

    Credit Hours: 3
    Overview of national and global soil/water resources, historical and current soil erosion issues, governmental conservation programs, agricultural management techniques to protect natural resources: use of erosion prediction equations in soil and water management.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03234  
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03434 - Weed Science: Ecology and Control

    Credit Hours: 3
    Study and evaluation of weed competition concepts and the current and evolving methods of weed management.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03130  or BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113 .
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03437 - Soil Judging

    Credit Hours: 1
    Field experience in soil evaluation.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03234  
    Repeatable: Course may be repeated twice for a new experience for a total of 3 hours.

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • AGRI 03438 - Advanced Crop Production

    Credit Hours: 3
    A comprehensive course to understand and apply advanced agronomic thinking and decision-making with respect to crop production.

    Prerequisite(s): Must be a senior in last semester or school year prior to graduation.
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03440 - Introduction to Precision Agriculture

    Credit Hours: 3
    Overview of precision agriculture technologies and techniques, including soil sampling, global positioning and remote sensing: discussion of environmental, economic and agronomic benefits/limitations of technologies/techniques.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03442 - Practical Field Crops Evaluation

    Credit Hours: 1
    Experience and practice in identification, agronomic calculations, and applications of agronomic facts. Consent of instructor required.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03232  
Credit Hours: 1
  

  • AGRI 03443 - Precision Agriculture for Production

    Credit Hours: 3
    Understand sources of field variability and how it can be mapped using precision agriculture software. Understand different sources of variability and how to collect data to map and use application controllers to efficiently manage and apply inputs in a crop or pasture system.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03440  
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03444 - Precision Agriculture Services

    Credit Hours: 3
    Develop an understanding of customers’ needs when purchasing and applying crop inputs or precision agriculture services. Learn to communicate the benefits of managing water, spatial data requirements, estimating return on investment, and create a precision agriculture presentation for potential customers.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03440  
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03445 - Applied Research in Precision Agriculture

    Credit Hours: 3
    Development and implementation of a practical research project in the application of precision agriculture technologies, such as GPS, GIS, and/or other agricultural technologies, to real world agricultural problems/situations. Project findings will be professionally presented to interested parties/faculty.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03440  and GEOG 32365 .
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03448 - Principles of Spray Application and Technology

    Credit Hours: 3
    A comprehensive course to understand the principles of spray applications and learn the importance of nozzle selection, tank mix properties, application parameters and technology resulting in successful spray applications.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03292  
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03449 - Independent Study in Agronomy

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    For students who wish to intensify, enrich or specialize in the study of any aspect of agronomy under the direction of a staff member. Consent of instructor necessary.

    Repeatable: Course is repeatable with topic change for a total of 3 hours.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • AGRI 03530 - Soil Formation and Classification

    Credit Hours: 3
    Identification and evaluation of soil morphological characteristics; study of the form and dynamics of soil bodies across the landscape and soil-forming factors and processes; introduction to pedological features used in Soil Taxonomy.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03234  
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03534 - Plant Breeding

    Credit Hours: 3
    A study of the improvement of agricultural plants by application of the principles of heredity.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03130  or BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113 .
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
