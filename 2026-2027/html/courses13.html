Military Science
  

  • MILS 42300 - Leadership and Management I

    Credit Hours: 3
    Provides the student with an examination of ethics, career professionalism, attributes of leadership, and selected light infantry tactical skills.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • MILS 42310 - Small Unit Leadership and Tactics

    Credit Hours: 3
    Course studies principles and fundamentals of military operations, the decision-making process, planning and execution of light infantry operations, and a thorough examination of military ethics.

    Prerequisite(s): MILS 42300  and advanced-course status.
    Corequisite(s): MILS 42312  
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • MILS 42400 - Leadership and Management II

    Credit Hours: 3
    Study of the principles of decision making, the planning and conduct of unit training, the role of the military in the U.S., professional ethical standards, and the use of those standards in situations involving power, influence, and subordinate counseling.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • MILS 42410 - Army Management and Organizational Systems

    Credit Hours: 3
    Advanced study of leadership and management that examines administration at the company level and military justice and an overview of the obligations and responsibilities of an officer.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • MILS 42450 - Independent Study in Military Science

    Credit Hours: 1 to 5
    Investigates selected leadership problems or topics on an individual or conference basis.

    Prerequisite(s): Consent of school chairperson.
    Repeatable: Course may be repeated with a change in topic.

Credit Hours: 1 to 5

    Credit Hours: 3

  • MUS 19225 - Jazz Improvisation

    Credit Hours: 2
    Designed to enable students to understand the basic concepts of jazz improvisation and to utilize the basic tools of jazz improvisation in order to express themselves musically in the jazz idiom.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • MUS 19230 - Collaborative Piano Workshop Activity

    Credit Hours: 1
    This course is designed to improve students’ level of musicianship and skills in the art of keyboard collaboration. It will help piano students become well-rounded musicians. Playing the piano collaboratively is an organic and natural part of being a pianist. Students will learn the essential skills they need to become better performance partners. Students learn through collaboration with their fellow students in the vocal and instrumental areas in a class setting with individual coaching.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor or previous enrollment in MUS 19230 .
    Corequisite(s): MUS 19130  or MUS 19135  or MUS 19137  or MUS 19330  or MUS 19335  or MUS 19337 .
    Repeatable: This course is repeatable for new experiences.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • MUS 19280 - World Music

    Credit Hours: 3
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR MUSC 102

    World music surveys the music of cultures outside the Western art tradition, with an emphasis on understanding the music within the context of the culture. Areas of focus include Africa, Latin America, India, Japan, Native American, Indonesia, China, and others. The course includes the fundamentals of music as paralleled in Northwest Core music classes. Fulfills a Humanities component of The Northwest Core Requirements.

    Typically Offered: SP


    https://journeytocollege.mo.gov/finish/transfer-credit/core-42/
Credit Hours: 3

  • MUS 19299 - Music Recital Attendance

    Credit Hours: 1
    Attendance at recitals and concerts presented by the School of Fine and Performing Arts (Music) is required of each music major and minor. B.M.E majors must pass 7 semesters prior to graduation. B.A. majors must pass 8 semesters prior to graduation. Minors must pass 4 semesters prior to graduation. Course is for activity credit only.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1
