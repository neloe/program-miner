Theatre
  

  • THEA 43225 - Oral Interpretation and Textual Analysis

    Credit Hours: 3
    Introduces the student to principles of literary analysis and techniques of oral interpretation as the foundation for all performance experiences.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43230 - Acting

    Credit Hours: 3
    Provides training in fundamental principles and techniques of modern acting. Emphasis is given to the formation and development of the actor’s imagination, intellect, and overall working process.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43235 - Theatre Voice and Movement

    Credit Hours: 3
    A survey of and practice with multiple theatre movement and voice theories designed to develop student awareness and skill related to the body’s expressive potential.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43230  or permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: Even SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43258 - Stage Makeup

    Credit Hours: 3
    Acquaints the student with basic principles of the art and technique of makeup and assisting the actor in the development and projection of his or her character on stage.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43305 - Independent Practicum in Theatre

    Credit Hours: 1
    Involves focused and mentored participation in various practical aspects of play production and/or performance;

    Repeatable: May be repeated for a total of four semester hours for further mastery.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • THEA 43316 - Playwriting

    Credit Hours: 3
    Composition or original short plays, dramatic scenes and monologues. The course provides an examination of the elements, discipline and art of playwriting through analysis of established plays and creation of original short plays, dramatic scenes and monologues.

    Prerequisite(s): ENGL 10210  or THEA 43225  or permission of instructor.
    Cross-Listed: ENGL 10316 

    Typically Offered: Odd SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43330 - Advanced Acting

    Credit Hours: 3
    Concentrated study in character analysis and acting methods.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43230  or permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: Even SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43335 - Acting Period Styles and Techniques

    Credit Hours: 3
    Provides training in the performance of character roles from various periods of dramatic literature and genres.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43230  or permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: Odd SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43354 - Stage Lighting

    Credit Hours: 3
    Concerns the role that lighting has to play in production, the lighting designer’s place in the production process and the procedures involved in designing lighting for stage.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43110  and THEA 43213  or permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: Odd FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43373 - Directing

    Credit Hours: 3
    Discusses the basic function of a director in the production of a play including selection, interpretation, composition, movement, characterization, rhythm, design concept and actor training.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43110  and THEA 43230  or permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43383 - Stage and Theatre Management

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course will explore the duties, responsibilities, and technique of modern stage and theatrical managers.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: Odd SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43391 - Costuming

    Credit Hours: 3
    Examines the use of clothing and stage costumes by major periods through style and design.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43110  and THEA 43212  or permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: Even SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43395 - Scene Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    Provides the student with a basic knowledge of the techniques and methods for designing scenery for the theatre, opera, musical, ballet and television.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43110 , THEA 43211 , THEA 43221 , or permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: Even FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43401 - Special Topics

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Provides concentrated study in special areas within theatre. Topics vary semester to semester.

    Prerequisite(s): Prerequisites as announced.
    Repeatable: Repeatable with topics changing.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • THEA 43407 - History of Theatre I

    Credit Hours: 3
    An examination of theatre’s evolution from its origins through 1700 as a reflection of and influence on society.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43408 - History of Theatre II

    Credit Hours: 3
    An examination of western theatre trends from the 18th to the 21st century as a reflection of and influence on society.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43426 - Interpreter’s Theatre

    Credit Hours: 3
    Provides study and practical application in selecting and adapting literature for group reading. Culminates in a public performance.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43440 - Preparing Repertory

    Credit Hours: 3
    Provides instruction and training in the methods required for preparing a repertory theatre season.

    Repeatable: May be repeated for a total of nine semester hours for further mastery.

    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43445 - Summer Repertory Theatre

    Credit Hours: 3
    Provides instruction and training in the various activities involved in the operation of a repertory theatre company.

    Repeatable: May be repeated for a total of nine semester hours for further mastery.

    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43460 - Creative Dramatics

    Credit Hours: 3
    Introduces the methods, techniques, and rationale for teaching and using creative dramatics activities with students from pre-school through secondary grades.

    Typically Offered: Odd FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43468 - Internship in Theatre Performance

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Professional experience in theatre performance. The particular interests of the students are addressed through practical application with theatre practitioners.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable for new experience.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • THEA 43469 - Internship in Technical Theatre

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Professional experience in technical theatre. The particular interests of the students are addressed through practical application with theatre practitioners.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable for new experience

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • THEA 43497 - Theatre Capstone

    Credit Hours: 3
    Concentrates on the development of the theatre professional. Topics include ethics; career planning strategies; internships, graduate school and advanced training opportunities; union membership; parallel industry careers; and emerging trends in the performing arts. This course includes a formal presentation of the student’s professional portfolio and preparation for public performance or exhibition of research, methods and practices comprising the student’s area of emphasis in theatre (senior project).

    Prerequisite(s): Advanced standing and permission of school chairperson.
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • THEA 43499 - Senior Project

    Credit Hours: 1
    A public performance or exhibition of materials comprising the student’s area of emphasis in theatre.

    Prerequisite(s): THEA 43497  or concurrent enrollment or permission of the instructor.
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • THEA 43501 - Special Topics

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Provides concentrated study in special areas within theatre. Topics vary semester to semester. Pre-requisites as announced.

    Repeatable: Repeatable with topics changing.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • THEA 43509 - Advanced Theatre Production

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Involves a practical approach to the art of producing a play. Specialized elements in mounting a play for production are discussed, relating to the specific needs of the students enrolled. The course culminates in a public performance.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for further mastery.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • THEA 43572 - Independent Study in Acting

    Credit Hours: 1 to 2
    Provides individualized, in-depth study in acting. This study is intended to develop and theoretically apply various principles of theatre arts to educational and professional theatre depending on the needs of the students.

    Repeatable: Repeatable with topics changing.

Credit Hours: 1 to 2
  

  • THEA 43573 - Independent Study in Directing

    Credit Hours: 1 to 2
    Provides individualized, in-depth study in directing. This study is intended to develop and theoretically apply various principles of theatre arts to educational and professional theatre depending on the needs of the students.

    Repeatable: Repeatable with topics changing.

Credit Hours: 1 to 2
  

  • THEA 43574 - Independent Study in Dramaturgy

    Credit Hours: 1 to 2
    Provides individualized, in-depth study in dramaturgy. This study is intended to develop and theoretically apply various principles of theatre arts to educational and professional theatre depending on the needs of the students.

    Repeatable: Repeatable with topics changing.

Credit Hours: 1 to 2
  

  • THEA 43575 - Independent Study in Technical Theatre

    Credit Hours: 1 to 2
    Provides individualized, in-depth study in technical theatre. This study is intended to develop and theoretically apply various principles of theatre arts to educational and professional theatre depending on the needs of the students.

    Repeatable: Repeatable with topics changing.

Credit Hours: 1 to 2
