Agriculture: Agronomy
  

  • AGRI 03536 - Soil Fertility

    Credit Hours: 3
    Principles of soil productivity and nutrients required for crop growth; fertilizer sources and nutrient reactions in soil; methods of fertilizer nutrient placement in major tillage systems; interpretation of soil test and plant analyses for determining crop nutrient requirements.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03234  
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03537 - Plant Genetics and Biotechnology

    Credit Hours: 3
    The application of modern molecular techniques to crop improvement. A study of crop improvement and characterization of agricultural plants through the application of plant genetics, genomics, and biotechnology.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03130 , or BIOL 04102  and BIOL 04103 , or BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107 .
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03539 - Soil Physics and Field Management

    Credit Hours: 3
    A survey of soil physical properties and processes important to land stewardship.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03234  
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03540 - Grain Crops

    Credit Hours: 3

    Prerequisite(s): 12 hours of agriculture.
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • AGRI 03543 - Soil Chemistry: Environmental Quality and Plant Nutrition

    Credit Hours: 3
    A comprehensive study of chemical processes in soil systems.

    Prerequisite(s): AGRI 03234  with a grade of C or better or instructor’s consent.

Art: Art Education
  

  • ART 13180 - Foundations in Art Education

    Credit Hours: 3
    Students will examine the social and cultural influences that impact teaching and learning in today’s schools. Students will learn the foundations of teaching art including introduction to art methods, the history of art education, curriculum development, culturally responsive pedagogy, assessment practices and the conceptual framework for national and state standards. Candidates will learn how to differentiate for different learners and participate in 20 hours of relevant field experience.

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13235 - Intersections in the Arts

    Credit Hours: 3
    Exploration of fundamentals, histories, cultures, and contemporary development in the fine and performing arts, which include the visual arts, music, theatre, and dance. Designed using an interdisciplinary structure, the course features and synthesizes visual, auditory, and kinesthetic experiences and applications. Fulfills Fine Arts component of The Northwest Core requirements.

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13280 - Visual Art Concepts

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course is an exploration of visual art history, theory, and criticism. Students examine art history through a global, contemporary lens. Students practice art theory by analyzing visual art in terms of form, content, and contextual evidence. Students practice art criticism by studying works of internationally recognized visual art critics and artists. The course emphasizes applying global themes from art history, art theory, and art criticism to written, verbal, and visual forms of communication.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  with a grade of C or higher.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13380 - Art in the Elementary School

Credit Hours: 2

Art: Art History
  

  • ART 13109 - Survey of Art I: Prehistory through Medieval Art

    Credit Hours: 3
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR ARTS 101

    A survey of the major monuments and themes of global ancient and medieval art, architecture, and visual culture. This course explores the production of artworks and architecture in diverse media from 35,000 BCE to 1300 CE in their artistic, cultural, and historical contexts.

    Typically Offered: FA


    https://journeytocollege.mo.gov/finish/transfer-credit/core-42/
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13111 - Survey of Art II: Renaissance to the Present

    Credit Hours: 3
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR ARTS 102

    A survey of the major protagonists, monuments, and themes of global art, architecture, and visual culture from the 14th to the beginning of the 21st century. This course introduces students to the basic art historical methods of visual and comparative analysis with the aim of establishing critically-based connections between artistic styles, techniques, and cultural and historical contexts.

    Typically Offered: SP


    https://journeytocollege.mo.gov/finish/transfer-credit/core-42/
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13308 - African American Art and Artists

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course explores the history and visual art that was created by Black artists in the United States from 1792 to present. It includes art history, formal and contextual art criticism, aesthetics, art appreciation, and a chronology of the unique stories of Black artists and their struggles with civil rights in this country.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  and ART 13111 .
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13311 - Ancient and Medieval Art

    Credit Hours: 3
    The history of ancient and medieval art, architecture, and visual culture from 3000 BCE to 1300 CE. Emphasis is placed on probation, reception, and intention, as well as ideological, social, and economic contexts. Subject areas covered included: Egypt, Aegean, Greece, Rome (including ancient Jewish and early Christian art), Medieval, Byzantine, Romanesque, and Gothic.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  and ART 13111 , both with a minimum grade of C, or permission of instructor for non-majors/minors.
    Typically Offered: Even SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13312 - Graphic Design History

    Credit Hours: 3
    Graphic Design history since 1890 or the Modernist and Information Era, with some key historical events, technology and persons prior to the 19th century. Includes graphic art developments and how these effected the 1890-1955 era of the emerging industry of graphic design, as well as the rise of digital visual aesthetics. There will be connections made between contemporary art and pivotal schools like the Bauhaus. Studies will link graphic tools like film and later the computer to graphic design. Topics will include print, mass media, typographers, photography, corporate graphics, the internet and post modern reactionary design with accompanying technology.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13111  and ART 13315 .
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13313 - Renaissance through Early Modern Art

    Credit Hours: 3
    An examination of the major themes and protagonists in art, architecture, and visual culture from the 14th through the mid-19th century, or from the Renaissance through Realism and Impressionism. The course emphasizes in-depth visual and theoretical analysis of artworks as well as comparative cultural study with close attention paid to social, historical, political, and religious contexts.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  and ART 13111 , both with a minimum grade of C, or by permission of instructor for non-majors/minors.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13315 - Nineteenth and Early Twentieth Century Art

    Credit Hours: 3
    A study of the major visual arts in Europe from the latter 18th century to the second decade of the 20th century.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  and ART 13111 .
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13317 - Modern and Contemporary Art

    Credit Hours: 3
    An examination of the major themes and protagonists in art, architecture, and visual culture from the late 19th century to the present, or from Post-Impressionism to Contemporary. The course emphasizes in-depth visual and theoretical analysis of artworks as well as comparative cultural study, with close attention paid to social, historical, political, and ideological contexts.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  and ART 13111 , both with a minimum grade of C, or permission of instructor for non-majors/minors.
    Typically Offered: Odd FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13318 - Asian Art

    Credit Hours: 3
    The history of art, architecture, and visual culture of Asia and South Asia. The production, reception, and diffusion of selected art forms over time will be examined using a variety of analytical methodologies in order to understand their significance in an Asian cultural context, and in relation to the history of Western interaction with Asian art.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  and ART 13111 , both with a minimum grade of C, or permission of instructor for non-majors/minors.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13322 - Art of the Islamic World

    Credit Hours: 3
    The history of art, architecture, and visual culture of the Islamic world. The course focuses on selected moments and monuments in the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and South Asia. An emphasis is placed on the relationship of visual arts to the history, geography, and traditions of each region as well as the cross-cultural connections between East and West in the development and transmission of artistic trends in Islamic art.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13109  and ART 13111  both with a minimum grade of C, or by permission of instructor for non-majors/minors.
    Typically Offered: Odd SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13411 - Special Topics in Art

    Credit Hours: 1 to 4
    This course provides concentrated study in special topics within Art History. Topics vary semester to semester.

Art: Ceramics
  

  • ART 13161 - Ceramics

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to studio pottery through comprehensive experience with clay preparation, basic forming, decorating, glazing methods, and kiln firing procedures at low temperatures. Basic studio work principles and individual design are stressed.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13302 - Wheel Throwing and Functional Form

    Credit Hours: 3
    An advanced-level course in ceramics. Emphasis on the fundamentals of working on the potter’s wheel and developing understanding of functional ceramic forms. Continued study of the physical properties of ceramic materials.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13161  
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13360 - Advanced Ceramics

    Credit Hours: 3
    Continued explorations in materials, processes and studio practice in ceramics. Assigned clay and glaze research as well as research into experimental uses of ceramic materials. Focused studio practice and development are stressed.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13161 , ART 13192 , ART 13302 , advanced standing and permission of instructor.
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13369 - Ceramics Studio

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Further cultivation of each student’s approach to materials and processes in ceramics. Mature, disciplined studio practice and focused development of a unique and resolved body of work are stressed.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13360  and permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 15 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 2 to 6
  

  • ART 13569 - Ceramics Processes

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Advanced studio work providing students the opportunity to further develop work in ceramics. Study in all phases of studio practice.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 6 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 2 to 6

    Credit Hours: 3
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR PERF 105D

    An introduction to and the application of basic principles of art in drawing using a variety of media, techniques and subjects.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

  • ART 13221 - Life Drawing

    Credit Hours: 3
    Application of the fundamentals of drawing to develop an understanding of creative composition using the human figure.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120  (art majors must complete with at least a grade of C).
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13321 - Advanced Drawing

    Credit Hours: 3
    A continued study of the human figure, other subjects and composition. More emphasis given to independent decision making.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13221 
    Typically Offered: SP, SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13429 - Drawing Studio

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Advanced exploration of drawing media, techniques, and concepts from representation to abstraction.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13321 , advanced standing and permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 15 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study. Art majors must complete with at least a grade of C.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 2 to 6
  

  • ART 13529 - Drawing Processes

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Advanced studio work providing students with opportunities for extended studies in various media and directions.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 6 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 2 to 6

Art: Graphic Design
  

  • ART 13191 - 2D Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    Introduction to two-dimensional design elements and principles, including design objectives, concepts, processes and skills through varied class projects and lecture/demonstration material.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13292 - Introduction to Digital Art Studio

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to digital media for creative imaging using computers, scanners, and digital photography. Appropriate hardware and software exploration for fine art purposes.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120  and ART 13191  (Digital Media majors may take concurrently with ART 13191  and are not required to have ART 13120  as a prerequisite; art majors must complete prerequisites with at least a grade of C).
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13294 - Typography

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course covers the study of typography from its fundamental principles, history, and terminology to creative visual expression and exploration of digital typefaces.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13191 . Graphic Design majors must pass ART 13191  with at least a grade of C.
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13388 - Interface Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course will explore front-end interactive design creation for creative professionals. Emphasis will be placed on crafting web and app prototypes, designing around CMS frameworks, and overall visual user interface and experience.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13292  and ART 13294 .
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13390 - Sequential Art

    Credit Hours: 3
    An exploratory of creative sequential art with an emphasis on storytelling i.e., storyboarding, comics and graphic novels, etc. This course will explore both printed and digitally reproduced arrangements of artwork and text (generally in the form of word balloons and captions).

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120 , ART 13221 , and ART 13292 .
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13394 - Advanced Digital Art Studio

    Credit Hours: 3
    Course covers advanced visual perception principles and computer procedures for creative digital imagery as it applies to interactive and design applications.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13292  with a minimum grade of C and advanced standing.
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13396 - Brand Identity Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    A course with an intensive approach to the research, creation, clarification, presentation and implementation of identity systems and branding.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120 , ART 13294  each with a minimum grade of C and advanced standing.
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13398 - Print and Digital Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    Graphic arts and design course for technical print and related media plus advanced interactive media graphics. Equal emphasis on creative problem solving and technical software application. Primary software applications involve desktop publishing and related production software.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120  and ART 13396 .
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13401 - Graphic Design Senior Capstone

    Credit Hours: 1
    A program capstone course for Graphic Design and Digital Media: Visual Imaging majors. The course will cover topics related to professional practices and career preparation in the graphic design and digital media professions. Emphasis will be placed on resume building, interviewing, personal branding, portfolio creation, web presence, networking, self-marketing, and professional development.

    Prerequisite(s): Junior or senior standing and permission of school chairperson.
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • ART 13488 - Package Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    Principles of graphic design applied in three dimensions. Projects explore principles of package design systems, materials, processes, sustainability, etc.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13394  and ART 13396 .
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13491 - Interactive Design

    Credit Hours: 2
    Interactive art/design theory with practical application of time-based motion and static graphics.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13394  or permission of instructor.
Credit Hours: 2
  

  • ART 13492 - Advertising Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    The creation of a full-service advertising campaign from research to finished art and presentation. Principles of research, market, consumers, strategy, positioning and branding are emphasized, as well as history and study of contemporary advertising issues.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13221 , ART 13396  and ART 13398 .
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13493 - Interactive Portfolio

    Credit Hours: 1
    Interactive design application of time-based motion and static graphics for CD-ROM portfolios or Web portfolios.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13191 , ART 13292 , ART 13394 , ART 13491 .
Credit Hours: 1
  

  • ART 13497 - Motion Graphics

    Credit Hours: 3
    Principles and procedures of creative digital animation.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13394  with a minimum grade of C.
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
