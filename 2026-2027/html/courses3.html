Art: Graphic Design
  

  • ART 13498 - Illustration

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course confronts drawing issues unique to a digital environment. 2D imagery will be the primary spatial focus but the class may also include virtual 3D illustration problems. Descriptive and interpretive drawing will be studied. Digital tools and design applications will be the primary focus.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120 , ART 13221 , ART 13292  each with a minimum grade of C.
    Typically Offered: Odd FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13499 - Design Studio

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Exploration of two- and three-dimensional graphic design processes using a variety of non-traditional materials.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13396  and permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 15 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 2 to 6
  

  • ART 13592 - Internship

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    To provide the student with an opportunity to work within a business context, so that the student can gain professional experience that would otherwise not be obtained in regular coursework.

    Prerequisite(s): Must be junior/senior level graphic design or IDM-VI major with a 3.00 GPA. Approval of work site by your instructor is required.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 6 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 2 to 6

Art: Painting
  

  • ART 13240 - Painting

    Credit Hours: 3
    A continued study of elements and principles of composition and color perception; synthesis of drawing and painting through work from traditional genre with concern for materials and methods in the use of the oil medium.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120  and ART 13191  (art majors must complete prerequisites with at least a grade of C).
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13340 - Advanced Painting

    Credit Hours: 3
    Emphasis on life study and exposure to alternative forms and media combined with an introduction to the conceptual side of painting.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13221 , ART 13240  and advanced standing.
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13343 - Watercolor Painting

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to both transparent and opaque watercolor with emphasis on their unique qualities and expressive potential.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13240  and advanced standing.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13449 - Painting Studio

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Advanced exploration of painting media, techniques and concepts, from representation to abstraction.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13340  with a grade of C or better, advanced standing, and permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 15 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study. Art majors must complete with at least a grade of C.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 2 to 6
  

  • ART 13549 - Painting Processes

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Advanced studio work providing students with opportunities for extended study in various media and directions.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 6 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 2 to 6

Art: Photography
  

  • ART 13201 - Creative Photography

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to the history of photography, camera operation, and photographic editing techniques. Photography as a creative art and expressive medium will be stressed.

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13207 - Digital Photography

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introductory creative photography course for students interested in digital media, and for students in the interactive digital media and graphic design majors. Students will explore contemporary applications of visual space and plastic form through the medium of electronic photography. Not for art majors.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13303 - Advanced Creative Photography

    Credit Hours: 3
    A continuation of ART 13201 . Students will develop a personal portfolio of expressive work, learn advanced photography techniques, and work on professional skills.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13201 
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13305 - Advanced Digital Photography

    Credit Hours: 3
    An exploratory continuation of creative photography with an emphasis on advanced digital photography, software, and printing applications. Photography as an art and expressive medium will be stressed.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13303  or ART 13207  and advanced standing.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13309 - Photography Studio

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Directed study and exploration of black and white, color, non-traditional and/or digital photography.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13303  and permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 15 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 2 to 6
  

  • ART 13509 - Photography Processes

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Advanced directed studies in various techniques in black and white or digital photography.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 6 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 2 to 6

Art: Sculptural/Three Dimensional Design
  

  • ART 13150 - Jewelry and Metalsmithing

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course is designed as an introduction to the language and techniques of jewelry and metalsmithing. During the course, students will focus on techniques and design concepts that deal primarily with the process of small-scale metal fabrication. Emphasis will be placed on jewelry and metalsmithing as an art form, not a means for production.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13192 - Three-Dimensional Design

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to the fundamentals of three-dimensional design explored through assigned problems and a variety of construction methods.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13120  and ART 13191  (completed with at least a grade of C for art majors).
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13270 - Sculpture

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to the basic concepts and techniques utilized in the production of sculpture, including additive/subtractive methods, welding, and site installation.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13192  (completed with at least a grade of C for art majors).
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13470 - Advanced Sculpture

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to concepts and techniques beyond those explored in ART 13270 . Emphasis will be on the execution of ideas and development of the student’s artistic direction.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13270  and advanced standing.
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • ART 13479 - Sculpture Studio

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Designed to allow students to focus more comprehensively on a particular direction or technique utilized in the production of sculpture. Discussions and assigned research on contemporary modes in sculpture.

    Prerequisite(s): ART 13470  and permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 15 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 2 to 6
  

  • ART 13579 - Sculpture Processes

    Credit Hours: 2 to 6
    Advanced studio work facilitated through discussions on contemporary concepts in sculpture, directed research and group critiques. Emphasis on student’s understanding of the content of his/her work and where that fits into the continuum of sculpture.

    Prerequisite(s): Permission of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable, up to 6 credit hours, for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 2 to 6

Biological Sciences
  

  • BIOL 04102 - General Biology

    Credit Hours: 3
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR BIOL 100L

    A general course in biology providing a broad understanding of the basic principles of biological science such as cells, energy production, photosynthesis, genetics, plant and animal physiology, ecology, environmental issues, and diversity. Upon completion, students will understand the relationship between living organisms and their environment and make more informed decisions regarding issues facing our society.

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04103 
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

  • BIOL 04104 - Medical Terminology

    Credit Hours: 3
    Medical terms encountered in the anatomy, physiology and surgical procedures of the life support systems.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04105 - Emergency Medical Dispatcher

    Credit Hours: 1
    This 30-hour course provides the theory and practice of emergency medical techniques used by dispatchers over the phone to assist 911 callers in maintaining life until emergency medical personnel arrive. This course includes American Heart Association Basic Life Support training and certification.

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • BIOL 04106 - Principles of Biology

    Credit Hours: 3
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR BIOL 150L

    A course designed for students who want more advanced study in the biological sciences which provides a broad survey of the fundamental principles of living systems, including their nature, processes, organization, and evolution. This course covers topics basic to the study of biology, including chemistry of biomolecules, prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell structure and function, cell division, classical and molecular genetics, evolutionary theory, general taxonomic classification, ecological principles, as well as the process of scientific inquiry.

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04107 
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP


    https://journeytocollege.mo.gov/finish/transfer-credit/core-42/
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04107 - Principles of Biology Laboratory

    Credit Hours: 1
    MOTR Equivalent: MOTR BIOL 150L

    A two hour companion laboratory which must be taken concurrently with BIOL 04106 .

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04106 
    Typically Offered: FA, SP


    https://journeytocollege.mo.gov/finish/transfer-credit/core-42/
Credit Hours: 1
  

  • BIOL 04108 - Emergency Medical Responder

    Credit Hours: 2
    Emergency Medical Responder certification is the first of four levels of pre-hospital emergency care certification/licensure in the US. This 40-hour course provides theory and practice of life support skills and techniques necessary to maintain life in emergency medical situations. The EMR certification is required of all law enforcement, firefighters, and rescue squad personnel.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04109 - Emergency Medical Technician-Basic 1

    Credit Hours: 3
    Theory and practice which allows one to gain and apply knowledge about the life support systems encountered in emergency medical situations. Students will be presented symptoms, treatment, practical experience and use of emergency medical equipment. This is the first of two courses that fulfill the requirements to take the National Registry Emergency Medical Technician examinations necessary to become a licensed Emergency Medical Technician-Basic.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04110 - Emergency Medical Technician-Basic 2

    Credit Hours: 3
    Theory and clinical practice which allows one to gain and apply knowledge about the life support systems encountered in emergency medical situations. Students will be presented symptoms, treatment, practical experience and use of emergency medical equipment. Students will participate in clinical experiences in both the ambulance and hospital emergency room. Clinical participation requires passing a background and drug test. This is the second of two courses that fulfill the requirements to take the National Registry Emergency Medical Technician examinations necessary to become a licensed Emergency Medical Technician-Basic.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04109  with a minimum grade of C.
    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04111 - Emergency Medical Technician-Basic Refresher

    Credit Hours: 2
    A 48-hour refresher course for licensed Emergency Medical Technician-Basics which provides required continuing education credits to maintain National Registry Emergency Medical Technician certification and Missouri State licensure.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04112 - General Botany

    Credit Hours: 3
    A fundamental study of plants and plant-like organisms. Topics include structure, physiology, evolution, diversity, classification, ecology and economic importance.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107  with a minimum of grade of C.
    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04113 
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture per week

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04113 - General Botany Laboratory

    Credit Hours: 1
    A two-hour laboratory which must be taken concurrently with BIOL 04112 .

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04112 
    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • BIOL 04114 - General Zoology

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introductory course on the life histories of animals taught using an evolutionary paradigm. Topics covered include taxonomy, diversity, anatomy, physiology, ecology, and reproductive strategies of major animal and protozoan clades.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107  with a minimum grade of C.
    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04115 
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04115 - General Zoology Laboratory

    Credit Hours: 1
    Two-hour laboratory which must be taken concurrently with BIOL 04114 .

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04114 
    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • BIOL 04140 - General Microbiology

    Credit Hours: 4
    A study of the morphology, physiology and cultivation of microorganisms. Studies on diversity, ecology, pathogenicity, fundamentals of immunology, applied microbiology and laboratory techniques will be included.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107 , or BIOL 04102  and BIOL 04103  with a minimum grade of C, and CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115 . Pre-nursing and dietetics students may substitute CHEM 24112  and CHEM 24113 .
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and four hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04210 - Current Topics in Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Each current topic is specifically designed to address a timely topic in biology.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04236 - Anatomy Allied Health

    Credit Hours: 4
    An introduction to the anatomy of the human body, including cells, tissues, organs, and organ systems. Comprehension is emphasized over memorization, but some basic terminology is also presented. This course is designed for beginning students in the allied health sciences, such as pre-nursing and pre-radiology.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04102  and BIOL 04103  or BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107 , and CHEM 24112  and CHEM 24113  or CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115 .
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04238 - Physiology Allied Health

    Credit Hours: 4
    The study of human physiological processes from the perspective of an allied health professional. Topics include muscle, nervous, cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, gastrointestinal, reproductive and endocrine physiology. Physiological responses to important medications and patient nutrient status will be emphasized.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04102  and BIOL 04103  or BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107  and CHEM 24112  and CHEM 24113  or CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115 .
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and three hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04261 - Local Flora

    Credit Hours: 2
    A two-hour laboratory course designed to acquaint the student with plants of this region and their classification.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113  with a minimum grade of C.
    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04293 - Medical Transcription and Medical Records Practicum

    Credit Hours: 2
    The practicum consists of the American Medical Association’s Medical Transcription Course which is a programmed study and directed practice in the medical records department. The directed practice includes experiences in admitting procedures, dismissing procedures, insurance forms, coding diseases and operations, medical record completion (inspecting charts for deficiencies), filing and computer data processing form completion.

    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture per week.

Credit Hours: 2

A professional paper describing the supervised experience will be required. By permission only. Three hours of internship may be counted towards the major or minor.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04302 - Internship: Organismal Biology

A professional paper describing the supervised experience will be required. By permission only. Three hours of internship may be counted towards the major or minor.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04303 - Internship: Ecological Biology

A professional paper describing the supervised experience will be required. By permission only. Three hours of internship may be counted towards the major or minor.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04307 - Environmental Internship

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Students will be placed in a work setting and become an active participant in an environmental area. 64 hours of on-site work will be required for each hour of credit. A professional paper describing the supervised experience will be required. By permission only.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04310 - Cell Biology

    Credit Hours: 4
    An introduction to the fundamentals of cellular structure and function. Cell physiology, molecular biology, cellular organelles, energy relationships and reproduction of cells are included.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107 , CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115  and CHEM 24116  and CHEM 24117  all with a minimum grade of C and sophomore standing.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): CHEM 24342  and CHEM 24343 .
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and three hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04312 - Invertebrate Zoology

    Credit Hours: 4
    A systematic treatment of major invertebrate phyla, classes and other groups, including taxonomic, anatomical, physiological, embryological and ecological features.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and two two-hour laboratories per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04318 - Principles of Taxonomy and Evolution

    Credit Hours: 4
    A study of the origin and diversity of life as well as both animal and plant classifications. Studies include contemporary systematic approaches, construction of keys, international rules of nomenclature, evolutionary principles, and origin of species.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113  and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and four hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: Even SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04330 - Animal Anatomy and Physiology

    Credit Hours: 4
    Introductory course dealing with basic anatomy and physiology of major animal groups. This course is taught using a systems-based approach, with emphasis on how anatomy and physiology correlates with the ecology, behavior, and basic life history of animals.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  and CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and two hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04348 - Biological Evolution and Diversity

    Credit Hours: 2
    A scientific survey of fundamental aspects of biological evolution and diversity. Topics covered include, but are not limited to, history of evolutionary thought, models of evolution, diversification, species concepts, adaptation and selection, and biogeography. These subjects will be illustrated with examples from the natural world, including prokaryote, protozoan, fungal, plant, and animal divisions of life.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113  and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115 .
    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04350 - Genetics

    Credit Hours: 4
    An introduction to the fundamental facts and principles of inheritance including molecular genetics, quantitative genetics, population genetics, and evolutionary genetics.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107  with a minimum grade of C and sophomore standing or permission of instructor.
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and two hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04362 - Mammalogy

    Credit Hours: 4
    A study of mammals with emphasis upon their classification, identification, evolution, life histories, ecology, habits, anatomy, physiology, and economic values.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113 , and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: One hour lecture and two three-hour laboratories per week.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04364 - Ornithology

    Credit Hours: 4
    A study of the habitats, life histories, structure, functions, evolution, ecology, classification and identification of the birds found primarily in this region.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113  and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: One hour lecture and three-hour laboratories per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04365 - Animal Behavior

    Credit Hours: 4
    A course that explores how and why animals act, covering topics including (but not limited to) foraging, communication, social structures, predator avoidance, and mating through lenses of evolution, genetics, ecology, and neurobiology. Students apply scientific methodology in the study of animal behavior and learn to understand the interplay of innate and learned behaviors, ultimately enhancing their ability to interpret animal interactions and promote animal welfare.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with grades of C or better.
    Hours per week: 3 hours of lecture and 2 hours of lab per week.

    Typically Offered: Odd FA

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04366 - Entomology

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to the world of insects: their structure and function, numbers, classification, life history, behavior, ecology and their relationship to humans.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113 , and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and one two-hour laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04368 - Survey of Wildlife Law

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introduction to federal and state regulations and issue associated with wildlife conservation and natural resource usage.

    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04376 - Basic Ecology

    Credit Hours: 4
    A basic study in ecological field techniques and ecological theory.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113  and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C or permission of instructor.
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and one three-hour laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04383 - Biology Practicum

    Credit Hours: 1
    Instruction and practical experience in development, teaching and the preparation of introductory biology laboratories. Coordinated by the bioscience coordinator. BIOL 04483  may be taken for an additional practicum credit.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112 , BIOL 04113 , BIOL 04114 , BIOL 04115 , and BIOL 04350  all with a minimum grade of C and junior standing or permission of instructor.
    Hours per week: One laboratory section per week.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • BIOL 04401 - Research: Cellular Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    This course is designed to give students experience in planning, conducting and presenting an undergraduate research project under the direction of a school faculty member. Topics may include, but are not limited to, microbiology, physiology, cell biology and genetics. Students may take up to 9 hours of research (BIOL 04401 , BIOL 04402 , and BIOL 04403 ) and internship (BIOL 04301 , BIOL 04302 , BIOL 04303 ) combined.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04402 - Research: Organismal Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    This course is designed to give students experience in planning, conducting and presenting an undergraduate research project under the direction of a school faculty member. Topics may include, but are not limited to, anatomy, botany, entomology, and zoology. Students may take up to 9 hours of research (BIOL 04401 , BIOL 04402  and BIOL 04403 ) and internship (BIOL 04301 , BIOL 04302 , BIOL 04303 ) combined.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04403 - Research: Ecological Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    This course is designed to give students experience in planning, conducting and presenting an undergraduate research project under the direction of a school faculty member. Topics may include, but are not limited to, ecology, environmental science, and wildlife conservation. Students may take up to 9 hours of research (BIOL 04401 , BIOL 04402  and BIOL 04403 ) and internship (BIOL 04301 , BIOL 04302 , BIOL 04303 ) combined.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04412 - Plant Anatomy and Morphology

    Credit Hours: 4
    A study of the development, structure and function of plant tissues and organs, as well as the form and structure of extinct plant groups found in the fossil record. Primary emphasis on vegetative and reproductive organs of gymnosperms and angiosperms.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  AND BIOL 04113 , and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and four hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: Alt SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04418 - Vertebrate Histology

    Credit Hours: 5
    The study of vertebrate tissues and organs. Laboratory consists of a microscopic study of cells, tissues, organs and organ systems.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04435  OR BIOL 04436  with a minimum grade of C.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04310 
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and six hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 5
  

  • BIOL 04420 - Environmental Issues

    Credit Hours: 4
    An overview of the science, politics and sociology of current environmental issues, including pollution, wastes, ozone depletion, acidic precipitation, greenhouse effect, deforestation, water use, energy and population.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107  with a minimum grade of C and CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115 .
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture plus one two-hour lab per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04429 - Problems in Cellular/Molecular/Microbial Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Focused study of a topic chosen by the student and faculty member. Topics may include, but are not limited to, microbiology, cell biology and genetics. Permission of instructor necessary.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04430 - Plant Physiology

    Credit Hours: 4
    A study of the chemical and physical processes involved in the growth and functioning of plants. Major topics include water relations, metabolic processes, and regulators of growth and development. Content includes material at the molecular, cellular, and organismal level.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113 , or BIOL 04310  or BIOL 04350 , and CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: Three hours lecture and three hours laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: Even SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04435 - Comparative Vertebrate Anatomy

    Credit Hours: 4
    An advanced course designed to familiarize students with fundamental and in-depth aspects of anatomy, evolution, and functional morphology in vertebrate animals. Laboratory includes hands-on dissection and examination of all major body systems in selected vertebrates. A course open to all students, but in particular an excellent option to prepare pre-veterinary students for veterinary school.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C and PHYS 25110  and PHYS 25111  OR PHYS 25120  and PHYS 25121  or permission of instructor.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and four hours of lab per week.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04436 - Human Anatomy

    Credit Hours: 4
    The systematic study of human anatomy, including the skeletal, muscular, cardiovascular, digestive, respiratory, urinary, endocrine, reproductive, and integumentary systems.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04104 
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and two hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04438 - Human Physiology

    Credit Hours: 4
    The study of the physiological processes of humans, including membranes, muscle, nervous, cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, gastrointestinal, endocrine and reproductive physiology.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04310  with a minimum grade of C.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04104  and either BIOL 04236  or BIOL 04432 or BIOL 04436 .
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and three hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04439 - Problems in Organismal Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Focused study of a topic chosen by the student and faculty member. Topics may include, but are not limited to, anatomy, botany, entomology, physiology, and zoology. Permission of instructor necessary.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04440 - Molecular Biology

    Credit Hours: 4
    An advanced course that explores the molecular structures, processes, and regulatory mechanisms related to DNA, RNA, protein expression and function.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04350  with a minimum grade of C.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04140 , BIOL 04310 
    Hours per week: Three hours of lecture and three hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: Odd SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04444 - Immunology

    Credit Hours: 5
    Principles of immunology: to include antigen-antibody relationships, host-antigen interaction, immunocytology, humoral and cellular response mechanisms, and serologic reactions.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04140  and CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115  with a minimum grade of C or permission of instructor.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and six hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 5
  

  • BIOL 04445 - Mycology

    Credit Hours: 3
    This course will examine the biology of the true fungi and other groups of organisms traditionally classified with the fungi. Topics covered will include taxonomy, life history traits, ecology, physiology, and evolutionary biology of the major classes and orders of fungi. Particular emphasis will be placed on the impact of fungi on human affairs. Laboratory exercises will emphasize the identification of these orders. One hour of lecture and three hours of laboratory per week.

    Recommended Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106 and BIOL 04107.
    Typically Offered: Odd FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04460 - Herpetology

    Credit Hours: 4
    Anatomy, physiology, taxonomy, distribution, life history and ecology of amphibians and reptiles, emphasizing those in Missouri.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Hours per week: One hour of lecture and two three-hour laboratories per week.

    Typically Offered: SP

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04470 - Readings in Ecology

    Credit Hours: 2
    Directed reading in ecology. Designed to acquaint the student with both historical and current developments in animal and plant ecology.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04376  with a minimum grade of C and senior standing.
    Hours per week: Two hours of discussion per week.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04510 - Current Topics in Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 6
    Each course is specifically designed to address a timely topic in biology.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 1 to 6
  

  • BIOL 04511 - Techniques in Biotechnology

    Credit Hours: 4
    A studio course dedicated to hands-on experience with common techniques used in the field of biotechnology. Theory and practical experience will be provided for techniques in DNA isolation and manipulation, gene cloning, library screening, molecular detection, and protein expression, genomic analysis and bioinformatics. In addition, students will be exposed to current topics and ethical considerations in the biotechnology industry.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04350  with a minimum grade of C.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04140  
    Hours per week: Two three-hour studio sessions per week with additional independent laboratory work required.

    Typically Offered: Odd FA

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04555 - Introduction to Bioinformatics

    Credit Hours: 3
    This will be a general introduction to bioinformatic tools and analytics. We will cover aspects of nucleic-acid and protein-sequence comparisons, assembly/annotation of small genomes, metabolic modeling of genomes, comparative genomics, phylogenetics, and computational biology.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04350  with a grade of C or higher.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and two hours of lab per week.

    Typically Offered: Even FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04576 - Methods in Plant and Animal Ecology

    Credit Hours: 3
    A studio course that provides a balanced survey of a variety of field techniques involving data collection or simulation and analysis of plant and animal populations and communities. Laboratory and field exercises will focus on critical thinking and problem-solving. This course is NOT designed to emphasize ecological theory nor scientific writing although aspects of each are included.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04376 
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): MATH 17114  for science majors highly recommended.
    Hours per week: Six hours of studio course times per week.

    Typically Offered: FA, First Block

Credit Hours: 3

Biological Sciences: Gulf Coast Research
  

  • BIOL 04351 - Marine Science I: Oceanography

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introductory course in oceanography which integrates chemical, geological and physical oceanography to provide fundamentals of oceanography at Gulf Coast Research Laboratory.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107  with a minimum grade of C and CHEM 24114  and CHEM 24115 .
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04352 - Marine Science II: Marine Biology

    Credit Hours: 3
    A general introduction to marine biology with emphasis on local fauna and flora at Gulf Coast Research Laboratory.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04106  and BIOL 04107  with a minimum grade of C and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  OR BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113  with a minimum grade of C.
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04353 - Marine Science Laboratory I

    Credit Hours: 2
    Field and laboratory exercises that accompany BIOL 04351 , consisting of various sampling techniques, analytical methods, data analyses, chart reading, and shipboard procedures.

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04351 
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04354 - Marine Science Laboratory II

    Credit Hours: 2
    Field and laboratory exercises that accompany BIOL 04352 , consisting of various sampling techniques, species identification, data analyses, chart reading, and shipboard procedures.

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04352 
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04450 - Coastal Ornithology

    Credit Hours: 3
    An introductory, field-oriented ornithology course that explores the highly diverse avian habitats found along the Mississippi Gulf Coast, emphasizing field ecology and behavior. Lectures, laboratory exercises, and field trips will introduce students to techniques including bird-banding, call-broadcast surveys, and other monitoring methodologies.

    Prerequisite(s): Two semesters of biology or permission of instructor.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): A course in general ecology.
Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04451 - Cetacean Behavior

    Credit Hours: 4
    Students will learn tools and techniques used in the systematic observation and documentation of dolphin and whale behavior in the wild. Course includes both classroom lecture and field studies focused primarily on dolphins of the Mississippi Sound.

    Prerequisite(s): Two semesters of biology or permission of instructor.
    Recommended Prerequisite(s): A course in general ecology.
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 4
  

  • BIOL 04453 - Shark Biology

    Credit Hours: 3
    An overview of elasmobranch biology with an emphasis on anatomy, physiology, and ecology of sharks, skates and rays. Lecture will cover such topics as diversity, evolution, anatomy and physiology, behavior, ecology, and fisheries management.

    Prerequisite(s): Three semesters of biology including BIOL 04352 - Marine Science II: Marine Biology  and BIOL 04354 .
    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04454  
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04454 - Shark Biology Laboratory

    Credit Hours: 2
    The laboratory will consist of elasmobranch identification and taxonomy, as well as anatomy and physiology through animal dissection. Several field trips will be taken to expose students to the various collecting methods used in field research.

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04453 
Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04455 - Marine Mammals

    Credit Hours: 3
    An overview of the biology of marine mammals (cetaceans, pinnipeds, sirenians, otters, and polar bear) including evolution and systematics, zoogeography, aquatic adaptations, life history and reproduction, population dynamics, diet and feeding, diving physiology and additional topics.

    Prerequisite(s): Three semesters of biology including marine biology or marine ichthyology or permission of instructor.
    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04456 
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04456 - Marine Mammals Laboratory

    Credit Hours: 2
    A companion field and lab course for Marine Mammals , emphasizing field identification and techniques.

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04455  
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04457 - Barrier Island Ecology

    Credit Hours: 2
    This course will familiarize students with concepts of coastal ecology with emphasis on the diversity of plant and animal communities unique to the northern Gulf of Mexico barrier island ecosystem.

    Prerequisite(s): Three semesters of science or permission of instructor.
    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04458 
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04458 - Barrier Island Ecology Laboratory

    Credit Hours: 2
    This course consists of field excursions to barrier islands off Mississippi and Florida coasts. Habitats to visit will include marsh and barrier island vegetation, aquatic and terrestrial invertebrates, mammals, birds and reptiles, brackish pond and lagoon communities, submerged seagrass communities, intertidal and shallow subtidal communities. Geologic processes and island dynamics will also be explored.

    Corequisite(s): BIOL 04457 
    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04471 - Environmental Photography

    Credit Hours: 3
    A course that develops an awareness of our environment through the medium of photography. Subjects include the structure and function of ecosystems, especially aquatic ones. Frequent field trips focus on selected environmental concerns. Lecture and practice will emphasize interrelationships at macroscopic and microscopic levels. Although no formal coursework is prerequisite, it is expected that students will have a basic awareness of environmental issues.

    Typically Offered: SU

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04474 - Wildlife Management and Conservation

    Credit Hours: 3
    A course designed to apply field and laboratory techniques to the management of game and nongame wildlife resources; management emphasis will entail conservation practices with consideration for threatened, rare and endangered species. Professionally written papers are required.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113 , BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115 , BIOL 04261 , and BIOL 04376  with a minimum grade of C and senior standing, or consent of instructor.
    Hours per week: Two hours of lecture and three hours of laboratory per week.

    Typically Offered: FA

Credit Hours: 3
  

  • BIOL 04478 - Readings in Molecular Biology

    Credit Hours: 2
    Directed readings in advances and techniques in molecular biology.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04310 , BIOL 04440  with a minimum grade of C.
    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 2
  

  • BIOL 04479 - Problems in Ecological/Evolutionary Biology

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Focused study of a topic chosen by the student and faculty member. Topics may include, but are not limited to ecology, environmental science, and wildlife conservation. Permission of instructor necessary.

    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
  

  • BIOL 04483 - Advanced Biology Practicum

    Credit Hours: 1
    An advanced course in practical procedures of instruction and preparation in biological laboratories. A maximum of two semester hours in advanced biology practicum courses is allowed.

    Prerequisite(s): Successful completion of assigned course that the student teaches as lab assistant plus consent of instructor.
    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

    Typically Offered: FA, SP, SU

Credit Hours: 1
  

  • BIOL 04489 - Problems in Biological Education

    Credit Hours: 1 to 3
    Permission of instructor necessary.

    Prerequisite(s): BIOL 04112  and BIOL 04113  and BIOL 04114  and BIOL 04115  with a minimum grade of C.
    Repeatable: Repeatable for additional experience, new material and progression of study.

Credit Hours: 1 to 3
